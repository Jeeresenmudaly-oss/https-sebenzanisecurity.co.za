Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Sebenzani Security handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or assessment request, we collect the organisation name, your name, telephone number, email address, service of interest and the message you provide.

Why we use it

We use these details to review and respond to your enquiry, discuss requested services, and keep records needed to manage the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Please do not submit access codes, security vulnerabilities, identification documents, or sensitive details about a site through this public form.

Who can access it

Enquiries are stored in Netlify’s form-submission service and may generate an email notification to the Sebenzani team. Access is limited to authorised team members who need the information to handle enquiries. Netlify and the team’s email provider process information to provide those services. These providers may process information outside South Africa; please refer to their current privacy terms for details.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry information for up to 12 months from submission, then delete it, unless a longer period is required to resolve an active matter or meet a legal obligation.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access or correct personal information, or raise a concern about how it is handled. Contact info@sebenzanisecurity.co.za. You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies and external services

This website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts; your browser may send technical connection information, such as your IP address, to Google to retrieve those fonts. The enquiry form is hosted by Netlify.

Keeping information safe

We use access controls and the security features provided by our website and service providers to protect enquiry information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please contact us promptly if you believe information you submitted has been exposed.
